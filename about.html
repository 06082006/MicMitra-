About Us

Beyond the Mic

It started with a feeling most of us know too well — the hand half-raised, then lowered. The answer we knew but didn't say out loud. The presentation we rehearsed perfectly in our head, and froze through in real life.

Growing up in Mysuru, I was taught to be curious, patient, and grateful — to really listen before speaking. Somewhere along the way, I also fell in love with the art of a good presentation: how the right words, said the right way, can make a room actually feel something. But I noticed most students never got the chance to find that out for themselves. Not because they had nothing to say, but because no one ever made it feel safe to say it.

That's where MicMitra began — not as a business plan, but as a question: what if confidence wasn't something you were born with, but something you built, together with people who felt the same fear?

The name says it simply.

Mic

Voice, speaking, confidence

Mitra

Friend, support, growth together



Put together, that's exactly what we try to be — not experts standing at the front of a room, but friends standing beside you, figuring it out together.

"We don't have to be experts to start. We become confident by doing it together."



That belief is why we don't lecture. We practice. We stumble through the same nerves our students do, and we grow louder, together, one session at a time.

Today, MicMitra is a small team of students — not professionals, not trainers, just people who remember what it felt like to stay silent, and decided to do something about it. We've run sessions so far, in classrooms and communities, and we're just getting started. Every session teaches us something new about what students actually need, and we carry that forward into the next room, the next voice, the next story.

This isn't a finished journey. It's one we're still walking, one conversation at a time — and we'd love for you to walk it with us.
